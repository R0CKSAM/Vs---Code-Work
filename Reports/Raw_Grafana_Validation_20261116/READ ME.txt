RAW GZIP / GRAFANA AUDIT - 16 NOVEMBER 2026

UTC-date folder 16 has 429,107 gzip files and 19,485,949 parsed records.
It does not cover the complete 16 November IST day by itself.
The adjacent UTC-date folder 15 is processed separately to supply the missing hours.

The final full-day window is 15 Nov 18:30 UTC <= request time < 16 Nov 18:30 UTC.
Only files from these local inputs are read. No shared lake partitions are modified.

After both local batches complete, finish_comparison.py combines their Parquet
parts without rereading the GZIP files and creates full_ist_day/comparison.html,
comparison.csv and comparison.json.

PROGRESS
utc16/progress.json and utc15/progress.json show checkpoints and errors.
parts/ contains private identifiers needed for exact distinct calculations.
Keep these scratch parts locally; they are ignored by Git. Shared results are aggregates.

DEFINITIONS
Eligible TS = raw path ends .ts, status 200/206, and filename ends -digits.ts or _digits.ts.
All-TS counts (any status) are reported separately.
Estimated watch hours are reported with both 0.6-second and 6-second assumptions.
Counts use the same exclusive end timestamp as Grafana.
Exact duplicate NDJSON lines are detected and retained, not silently removed.
Local exact identifier counts are not identical to Grafana approximate uniq counts.
Device/session parsing may differ from Hydrolix ingestion transformations.
All client IPs and IP+UA values remain local; no identifiers are returned by live queries.
