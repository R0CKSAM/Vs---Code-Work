Open the PDF for sharing. There are 14 slides, with presenter notes in PowerPoint.

Coverage: 18 Sep to 14 Nov 2026, 31 represented dates.
Headline: 3,422,658 distinct client IPs on positive raw_ts_requests rows.
Volume: 1,204,891,915 raw TS requests across six property labels.
These are delivery metrics, not people, paid-media impressions or watch minutes.
Event counts overlap; do not sum per-event IP counts.
The source proves a streaming portfolio, not event organisation or commercial rights.
Sponsorship packages and activations are proposals, not booked inventory.

Aggregate CSV and JSON files provide the calculation trail. No raw IPs/device IDs are exported.
