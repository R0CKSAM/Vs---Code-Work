Download and methodology

PNG chart · SVG chart · Content hourly CSV · Content daily CSV · Minute overlap CSV · Full analysis JSON

All three IST days include their required adjacent raw-date spillover files. STREAM and FAST are scanned separately; duplicate lake copies are resolved by the shared partition helper. No request-ID deduplication is applied: the measure is logged request rows. All hourly, feed, UA, status and minute totals reconcile to daily counts. Available previous reports are cross-checked. A feed absent within a fully scanned source-day is shown as zero.

The analysis does not infer watch hours, device ownership, intent, content completion or exact session concurrency.
