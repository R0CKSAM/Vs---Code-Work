Traffic by hour (segment requests)

Viewing is overwhelmingly non-primetime: the single biggest hour (16:00) carries almost as much traffic as the entire 19:00–24:00 block combined — consistent with an NRI/diaspora audience watching across scattered time zones rather than one evening peak.

Platform share

73% of all requests come from Android TV / FireTV — this is a living-room, big-screen product, not a phone-first one.

Resolution mix by platform

TV platforms pull noticeably higher bitrates than mobile: 91% of Android TV/FireTV segments are 1080p or 4K, vs iOS where 55% never rises above 720p — iOS sessions look constrained by network/ABR, not just screen size.

Top 10 states (India, 99.7% of traffic)

Delhi alone drives 31% of national requests from just 2,636 distinct viewers — the highest requests-per-viewer ratio of any major state, meaning Delhi viewers are watching noticeably longer sessions, not just more numerous.
