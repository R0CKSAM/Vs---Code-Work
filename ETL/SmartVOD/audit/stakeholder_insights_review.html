Available views

Executive brief, Regional trends, Rises & drops, Top audience, Change breakdown and How this is measured. Metrics: estimated watch hours, known period-distinct Device IDs and client IPs. Geography: country, state or city. Breakdown: device, OS, ISP, content title or category.

Questions route to measured analyses; they do not invoke an unrestricted language model or invent explanations. Examples: Which regions dropped? Trend in Uttar Pradesh. Which ISP increased? Top client IPs.

Evidence and safeguards

  • Equal calendar comparison windows, identical master filters and IST time windows.
  • Unavailable history remains unavailable; absent dates are gaps, not zero audience.
  • Unique IDs are computed across each period, not added across days or places.
  • Unusual daily changes use a disclosed median/MAD screening rule and need four prior loaded dates.
  • Each returned movement or alert has its own region trend; unknown geography is excluded from movement headlines.
  • Top audience is identifiers/IPs, not verified people. High estimated activity is a review flag, not proof of automation.
  • Exports include report totals, comparison dates, baseline kind and baseline coverage.
  • Insight filters refresh analysis only; graph refresh is deferred until Dashboard is opened.

Observed performance

The same 35-day analysis: 131.72 seconds on SQLite, 5.25 seconds on the compact columnar store. Full-history selection through the real date picker: 5.28 seconds. These are measured local runs, not service guarantees.

Projection: 6,780,267 rows, approximately 267 MiB. Source row/hour/request totals reconcile. The projection is accepted only for its recorded source fingerprint; changed snapshots use a safe fallback until rebuilt. Snapshot preparation refreshes acceleration automatically. Two analysis threads and a 1GB DuckDB memory limit.

Large historical Dashboard graph queries are a separate workload and can remain slow; Insights avoids that work while browsing analyses.
