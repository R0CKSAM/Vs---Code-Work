Important metric differences
  • Sponsor-deck users are distinct client IPs with positive TS counts. Grafana daily users can include broader traffic.
  • Deck hours use TS counts × 0.6 ÷ 3600. Existing Grafana templates use 6 seconds, a tenfold difference before other filters.
  • This Parquet has no HTTP status, request path, city, sessions, bandwidth or player timestamps. Those cannot be reconstructed.
  • Raw TS counts may include traffic excluded by Grafana’s HTTP 200/206 and filename filters.
  • Parquet device/OS classifications differ from Grafana’s UA-based rules. No automatic distinct-count rollup is made.
